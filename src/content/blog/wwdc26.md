# The new Siri

After nearly 15 years, Siri has gotten its first major refresh, and may even be intelligent now. When Siri AI comes out,
you will see Siri in Dynamic Island when triggered. Siri will also get a new app to be able to have in-depth
conversations and make sure they are saved.
